Dink Vision Recorder uses JavaCV 1.5.12 and JavaCPP 1.5.12 (Apache 2.0), Bytedeco FFmpeg 7.1.1-1.5.12 non-GPL Android builds (LGPL 3.0), and UVCAndroid 1.0.13 (Apache 2.0) with libusb (LGPL 2.1), libuvc (BSD), libjpeg-turbo, and libyuv. The accompanying GPL text is part of the LGPL license terms, not an indication that the GPL FFmpeg variant is used. Native libraries are dynamically linked. AndroidX uses Apache 2.0.
